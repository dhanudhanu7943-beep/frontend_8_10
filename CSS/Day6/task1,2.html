position: sticky; is a positioning method that acts as a hybrid between relative and fixed positioning based on the user's scroll position.
An element with sticky positioning behaves like a normal (relative) element in the document flow until the viewport scrolls to a
specific threshold, at which point it "sticks" in place (behaving like fixed). A sticky section stays fixed in place as visitors scroll through your page, making it ideal for keeping important content visible, such as promotions, navigation bars, or product details Sticky content is one that will stay positioned once the user scrolls past it (i.e: stuck in position). Unlike position: fixed; , sticky content is limited by its container and will stop scrolling once the end of the parent element is reached.
