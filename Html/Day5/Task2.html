A computer is an electronic device that accepts data, processes it, stores it, and produces useful information. Computers are used in schools, offices, hospitals, banks, and many other places. Computer use generally refers to the practical application of a computer system to perform tasks, manage information, or solve problems. It involves entering data (input), processing it according to specific software instructions, and generating a usable result (output).
