Inline define

Inline elements are HTML elements that do not start on a new line. They only take up the amount of space required by their content and are displayed within the same line as surrounding text or other inline elements. They are mainly used to format text, create hyperlinks, or add small pieces of content inside a block-level element such as a paragraph.

Underlined

The(underline) element is an inline element used to display text with an underline. It is commonly used to indicate non-textual annotations or to visually underline specific words or phrases.
