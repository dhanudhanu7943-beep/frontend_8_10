Company Name: Cognizant Technology Solutions

About Company

The company specializes in digital transformation, cloud computing, cybersecurity, and artificial intelligence engineering. It acts as a technology partner, helping global banks, healthcare networks, and retailers modernize their operations. Originally founded in Chennai in 1994, its global corporate headquarters is now in Teaneck, New Jersey. It is a Fortune 500 company traded on NASDAQ and employs hundreds of thousands worldwide. Chennai serves as its largest operational hub, housing massive tech campuses across major IT corridors.

Core Services

Data & AI: Development, fine-tuning, and scaling of full-stack AI solutions and training data services.

Digital Engineering: Modernizing legacy applications and engineering software platforms using tools like Cognizant Flowsource and Neuro AI.

Consulting: Management consulting, operating model design, and enterprise process optimization.

Cybersecurity: Holistic threat management and risk orchestration via the Neuro Cybersecurity suite.

Business Process Services: AI-powered customer engagement, global payroll management, and contact center operations.
